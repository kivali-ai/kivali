The Kivali logo: a tile with two honey-dotted i's, and a wordmark drawn from Space Grotesk 500 outlines with honey dots centered on each i and a slightly shorter final i. All files are SVG with fixed colors; they do not inherit `currentColor`.

- `kivali-lockup.svg`: icon plus wordmark, `ink` letters. The default logo on `paper` and `paper-raised`.
- `kivali-lockup-dark.svg`: the same with cream letters and a lifted tile, for dark grounds.
- `kivali-lockup-mono-ink.svg` / `kivali-lockup-mono-paper.svg`: icon plus wordmark in one color, the i's cut out of the tile. For embroidery, print and stamps.
- `kivali-icon.svg`: the icon alone on an `ink` tile. Favicons, avatars, social images; works on light and mid grounds.
- `kivali-icon-dark.svg`: the icon with a lifted tile (#2E2A23) so its edge shows on the dark `paper`.
- `kivali-icon-mono-ink.svg` / `kivali-icon-mono-paper.svg`: one color, i's cut out of the tile. For embroidery, print and stamps.
- `kivali-wordmark.svg` / `kivali-wordmark-dark.svg`: the wordmark alone, `ink` or cream letters with `signal` dots.
- `kivali-wordmark-mono-ink.svg` / `kivali-wordmark-mono-paper.svg`: the wordmark in one color, dots included.
- `kivali-icon-512.png`, `kivali-icon-180.png`, `kivali-icon-32.png`: raster icons for app stores, Apple touch icons and legacy favicons.
