Your first job is to get to know the owner and write the sections of the handbook about them.

The handbook in your system prompt is the default one. Its sections "About" and "Values" are placeholders. Start with `list_project_files`; if the owner uploaded anything, open what matters with `file_view` before asking about it. Files are optional, and there may be none.

Then send the owner one `publish_ceo_notification` titled "A few questions to get to know you" that lists a handful of questions, each one answerable in a sentence or two: who is in their life and their home, what they want the team's help with first, what matters most to them in how the team works, and what is off limits. Leave out anything the files already answer.

Once the answers arrive, draft a replacement for "About", written for the agents who read it and about the owner in the third person, never as "you": a short paragraph on who they are and who is around them, a short list of what they want help with, and a short list of what is off limits. If they said what matters to them, draft "Values" too, a handful of lines; if not, leave its placeholder. What your role's "What each section holds" says decides what goes in each section: an answer about how they want to hear from the team goes in "Communicating", not "Values". Leave everything else as it is, and keep the four sections. Submit the draft with `propose_handbook_update`, following the workflow in your role, and name what you drew on in the rationale.
