Your first job is to get to know the owner's business and write the sections of the handbook about it.

The handbook in your system prompt is the one setup wrote. Its sections "About" and "Values" are placeholders unless the owner filled them in during setup. Start with `list_project_files`; if the owner uploaded anything, open what matters with `file_view` before asking about it. Files are optional, and there may be none.

Then send the owner one `publish_ceo_notification` titled "A few questions to get to know the business" that lists a handful of questions, each one answerable in a sentence or two: what the company does and for whom, its stage and how it makes money, what they want the team's help with first, what matters most to them in how the team works, and what is off limits. Leave out anything the handbook or the files already answer. If nothing is left to ask, send a short introduction instead: what you read, and that you are ready for work.

Once the answers arrive, draft a replacement for "About", written for the agents who read it: a paragraph on what the company does and a paragraph on the business model and stage, as they stand today. If the owner said what the company values, draft "Values" too, a handful of lines; if not, leave its placeholder. What your role's "What each section holds" says decides what goes in each section: an answer about how they want to hear from the team goes in "Communicating", not "Values". Leave everything else as it is, and keep the four sections. Submit the draft with `propose_handbook_update`, following the workflow in your role, and name what you drew on in the rationale. Nothing in the draft is guessed: what the owner and the files did not say stays out.
